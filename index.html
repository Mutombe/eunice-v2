03 — A working ethic

Four beliefs
the studio works by.

Held quietly. Defended only by example.

  1. I

    A room is never decoration. It is a quiet collaborator.

  2. II

    Wellbeing is not a posture. It is an architecture.

  3. III

    Reinvention is not recovery. It is design.

  4. IV

    Restraint, in the end, is the most generous luxury.

— Founder

Eunice
De Campi.

b. Lagos · founded the studio in Oxford, 2008

Eunice De Campi is a coach, writer and interior wellbeing designer. The studio was founded in Oxford in 2008 and has grown — slowly, quietly — into a wellbeing-first practice for women in reinvention, with a small interiors arm and a quarterly editorial journal running alongside.

Aligned mindset coach, holistic wellbeing strategist, designer, writer — and mother. The disciplines are different doors into the same room.
